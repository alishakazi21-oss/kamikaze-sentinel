# VYOM-RAKSHAK: Sensor Reality Models & Mathematical Formulations

This document describes the physical sensor simulation models implemented in **VYOM-RAKSHAK** (ASTRA VEER). All formulations and parameters are unclassified, software-only approximations derived from standard radar, acoustics, and electro-optical engineering literature.

## 2. Active 3D Surveillance Radar Model

### 2.1 Radar Range Equation & SNR
The signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) for a monostatic pulsed Doppler radar detecting a target of radar cross section (RCS) $\sigma$ ($m^2$) at slant range $R$ ($m$) is modeled using the fourth-power range law:

$$\text{SNR}_{\text{linear}} = \left(\frac{R_{\text{ref}}}{R}\right)^4 \cdot \left(\frac{\sigma}{\sigma_{\text{ref}}}\right) \cdot L_{\text{env}} \cdot \eta_{\text{sensor}}$$

where:
- $R_{\text{ref}} = 3500\text{ m}$ is the textbook reference detection range for a standard $0\text{ dBsm}$ ($1.0\text{ m}^2$) target.
- $\sigma = 10^{(\text{RCS}_{\text{dBsm}} / 10)}$ is target radar cross section.
- $L_{\text{env}}$ is the environmental attenuation factor (rain loss, urban multipath, electronic jamming).
- $\text{SNR}_{\text{dB}} = 10 \cdot \log_{10}(\text{SNR}_{\text{linear}})$.

### 2.2 CA-CFAR Detection under Swerling I Fluctuations
Radar detection probability in thermal noise and homogeneous clutter with a Cell-Averaging Constant False Alarm Rate (CA-CFAR) detector and a slowly fluctuating target (Swerling Case I / Chi-Square with 2 degrees of freedom) is computed analytically via the closed-form textbook formula:

$$\alpha = P_{\text{FA}}^{-1/N} - 1$$

$$P_D = \left( 1 + \frac{\alpha}{1 + \text{SNR}_{\text{linear}}} \right)^{-N}$$

where:
- $P_{\text{FA}} = 10^{-4}$ is the design probability of false alarm per resolution cell.
- $N = 16$ is the number of independent CFAR reference training cells.
- $\alpha$ is the CFAR threshold scale multiplier.

A uniform random draw $u \sim \mathcal{U}(0, 1)$ derived from the deterministic scenario seed determines whether a detection plot is generated.

### 2.3 False Alarm Rate & Distribution
The expected false alarms per 360° radar volume scan is:

$$\lambda_{\text{FA}} = P_{\text{FA}} \times N_{\text{range\_cells}} \times N_{\text{beams}} \times K_{\text{clutter\_mod}}$$

The integer count of false plots per sweep is drawn from a seeded Poisson distribution:

$$k \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda_{\text{FA}})$$

Positions are uniformly distributed across range and azimuth coverage, flagged with `is_false_alarm = True` (accessible only in post-mission AAR debriefs).

### 2.4 Measurement Uncertainty & CRLB
In accordance with the Cramér-Rao Lower Bound (CRLB), measurement standard deviations shrink inversely with the square root of SNR:

$$\sigma_R = \max\left(\sigma_{R,\min}, \frac{\sigma_{R,\text{base}}}{\sqrt{\text{SNR}_{\text{linear}}}}\right)$$

$$\sigma_\theta = \max\left(\sigma_{\theta,\min}, \frac{\sigma_{\theta,\text{base}}}{\sqrt{\text{SNR}_{\text{linear}}}}\right)$$

## 3. Acoustic Phased Array Model (Bearing Only)

### 3.1 Transmission Loss & Noise
Acoustic noise generated by multi-rotor drone propulsion spreads spherically in air with atmospheric molecular absorption:

$$\text{SPL}(R) = \text{SPL}_0 - 20\log_{10}(\max(1.0, R)) - \alpha_{\text{abs}} \cdot R$$

$$\text{SNR}_{\text{dB}} = \text{SPL}(R) - N_{\text{ambient}} - \Delta_{\text{urban}}$$

where:
- $\text{SPL}_0 = 75\text{ dBSPL}$ at $1\text{ m}$ reference distance.
- $\alpha_{\text{abs}} = 0.005\text{ dB/m}$ at standard temperature and humidity.
- $N_{\text{ambient}} = 45\text{ dB}$ (rural) or $57\text{ dB}$ (urban traffic).

### 3.2 Logistic Detection Probability
Acoustic array beamforming detection is modeled via a logistic psychometric function:

$$P_D = \frac{1}{1 + \exp(-(\text{SNR}_{\text{dB}} - \gamma_{\text{sens}}))}$$

where $\gamma_{\text{sens}} = 6.0\text{ dB}$ is the $50\%$ threshold sensitivity of the array.

### 3.3 Bearing-Only Output
Acoustic sensors report **bearing only** ($\theta_{\text{meas}}$) without direct range measurement (`range_m = None`).

## 5. Engineering Simplifications & Assumptions
1. **Radar Antenna Pattern**: Assumed idealized sectoral pencil beams; side-lobe levels are approximated through the clutter multiplier.
2. **Atmospheric Ducting**: Multipath and ground bounce are abstracted into clutter injection and altitude noise rather than solving full parabolic wave equations.
3. **Target Acoustic Directivity**: Assumed omnidirectional source level for quadcopters; blade passage frequencies (BPF) are abstracted into composite SPL.
4. **Offline Laptop Constraint**: All models compute in $\mathcal{O}(N)$ closed form, executing in $<0.5\text{ ms}$ per scan without GPU acceleration.
